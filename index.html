About Our Centre

Built With Love, Dedicated To Senior Dignity

Maa Ka Ghar was established with a deeply personal mission: to create a warm, secure, and family-like alternative to institutional facilities. We understand that as parents age, they need more than just medical supervision—they need genuine emotional warmth, respectful companionship, and a peaceful environment.

Whether your loved one needs daily assistance with routine activities, recovery support after a hospital stay, or long-term assisted living, our staff treats every resident as an extended member of our own family.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is Maa Ka Ghar available for both male and female seniors?

Yes, we provide safe, dignified accommodation and dedicated attendants for both male and female elderly residents.

2. Do you provide 24/7 care assistance?

Yes, trained attendants are present 24/7 to assist residents with mobility, bathroom needs, medication administration, and emergency response.

3. What happens in case of a medical emergency?

We maintain established ties with nearby hospitals, conduct immediate doctor tele-consultations, notify family members instantly, and coordinate swift ambulance transport if required.

4. Can family members visit residents?

Absolutely! We encourage regular visits. Our general visiting hours are between 10:00 AM and 7:00 PM daily.

5. Can meals be customized according to doctor instructions?

Yes, our kitchen prepares customized diabetic, low-salt, or soft-diet meals as advised by medical professionals or requested by family.

6. Can seniors stay on a temporary or short-term basis?

Yes, we offer flexible Short Stay plans for post-surgery recovery or temporary care when primary caregivers at home are travelling.
